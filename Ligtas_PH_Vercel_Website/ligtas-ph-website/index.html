ABOUT LIGTAS PH

Built around a simple idea: readiness for everyone.

Disaster information can be scattered across agency websites, local announcements, and social media. Ligtas PH is a student venture concept that aims to make key information and preparedness resources easier to access through one mobile experience.

Our goal is to encourage preparedness and help users know where to look for reliable information when it matters.
